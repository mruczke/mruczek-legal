Privacy Policy: MRUCZEK 1.0

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Controller and sole user: MruczkeFOTO, Warszawa, Poland, contact: mruczkefoto@gmail.com (“I”, “the owner”).

1. What MRUCZEK 1.0 is

MRUCZEK 1.0 is a private, local back-office tool for my own photography business. It runs only on my own computer and is accessible only from my own devices over a private network. It is not offered to the public, and nobody except me can sign in to it. It connects to TikTok through the official TikTok API (Login Kit and Display API) to read statistics of my own TikTok account. It does not post, delete, edit, like, comment or send messages on TikTok.

2. Data accessed from TikTok

Only after I authorise the app with my own TikTok account, and only for the scopes I granted:

ScopeData
user.info.basicopen ID, display name
user.info.profileusername, verification status
user.info.statsfollower count, following count, likes count, video count
video.listmy public videos: ID, title, description, duration, share link, view, like, comment and share counts, publication time

The app requests only the fields listed above, even where a scope would allow more. It does not access private videos, other people’s accounts, followers’ lists, messages or contact lists, and does not collect data about other TikTok users.

3. Purpose

I use the data only to understand how my own content performs (for example, which videos get the most views) and to plan my own content. The data is not used for advertising, profiling of other people, resale or any purpose unrelated to my own business.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local database on my own computer, protected by the operating-system account. Access tokens are stored encrypted (the encryption key is kept in the operating-system keychain) and are never written to logs or shown in the interface. The app is not exposed to the public internet; remote access from my phone is possible only through a private Tailscale network and requires a one-time pairing code.

5. Sharing

I do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties. Data is not transferred to any other service by the app. When I ask my AI assistant to help me analyse my own statistics, the figures I choose to discuss are processed by the provider of that assistant under my own account and its terms; this concerns only my own account’s statistics.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on my computer until I delete it. When I disconnect TikTok in the app, the stored access token is deleted immediately. I can also revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services / Manage app permissions), after which the app can no longer read my data. Previously stored statistics can be deleted on request by removing them from the local database; to request deletion, contact me at mruczkefoto@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Because the app only processes data of my own TikTok account, I am both the controller and the person concerned. If you believe this app processes your personal data, you can contact me at mruczkefoto@gmail.com to ask for access, correction or deletion, and you can complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO) or your local authority. Under the GDPR the lawful basis is my legitimate interest in running my own business (Art. 6(1)(f)) and my own consent as the account holder (Art. 6(1)(a)).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

9. Changes

I may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.

TikTok is a trademark of its owner. MRUCZEK 1.0 is an independent tool and is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok.
